# Battle Fitness Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: 5 November 2026

**This is a draft written for the OPSC6312 project and must be reviewed by the app owners before it is published or submitted to Google Play.** Fields in [square brackets] must be completed.

Battle Fitness ("the app") is a fitness tracking and friendly-competition app made by Ethan Carr and Insaaf Behardien (students). It is not a commercial service.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address and a password (email sign-up) | To create your account and let you log in. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication, which stores only a hash. The app and its developers never see or store your password. |
| Google account name, email address and profile photo (if you use Google sign-in) | To create your account and show your name. |
| Username, first name, surname, birth date (email sign-up only), country, height, weight, preferred language and units | To run your profile, show you in leaderboards, and show measurements in your units. |
| Workouts you log (exercise, sets, reps, duration, notes, time) | To show your progress. Stored on your phone and synced to your account. |
| Clubs, fixtures, scores and proof photos or videos you upload | To run leagues between friends. |
| Profile picture and banner image you upload, and proof photos referees attach to a match | To show on your profile and in your clubs. They are shrunk, compressed and stored in the app's database (Cloud Firestore), and a copy is kept on your phone. |
| A notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | To send you notifications you have turned on. |

We do not collect your location, contacts or advertising ID. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging), which runs on Google Cloud servers. Workouts are also kept in a database on your phone so the app works offline.

## Who can see it

- Other members of a club you join can see your username, profile picture and your league results.
- Your email address, birth date, height, weight and workout notes are not shown to other users.
- We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

## Your choices

- You can edit your username, height, weight and country in **Settings**.
- You can turn notifications off in **Settings**.
- You can delete your account in **Settings, Account, Delete account**. This permanently deletes your sign-in, your profile document, your workouts and your notifications. Some history that belongs to a club (for example past fixture results involving you) may remain in that club until the club is deleted. [Confirm this when the club feature is finished.]

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13. We ask for a birth date when you sign up with email and refuse accounts under 13.

## Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by Firebase security rules so that users can only read and change what they are allowed to.
